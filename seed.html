DeliMi · Seed menus

One-time (or re-run) import of the transcribed menus into Firestore. Works from GitHub Pages or opened straight from your desktop. Sign in as a super admin first.

Re-running replaces the screens of every store listed in SEED_DATA.menus and upserts catalog items. Stores without a transcribed menu keep whatever screens admins built; only their active flag is synced (inactive stores are hidden from the store picker).
